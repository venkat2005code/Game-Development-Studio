// Life at PixelForge

Our Culture

Game Jam Fridays

Every other Friday is a voluntary game jam where teams build mini prototypes in 4 hours. The best ideas often become real features — or even full games.

Annual Studio Summit

Once a year, the whole studio flies to one city for three days of talks, workshops, team-building, and a full day of gaming together. Always memorable.

Pixel Talks

Monthly internal talks where any team member can share research, discoveries, or a game that inspired them. Knowledge sharing is in our DNA.
