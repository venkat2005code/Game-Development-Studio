// Our Story

Built by Players,
for Players.

PixelForge was founded in 2018 by a team of obsessed gamers who believed games could be more than entertainment — they could be art, culture, and legacy.

// Mission

Why We Forge Games

The rarest resource in game development isn't budget or technology — it's meaningful creative direction. PixelForge exists to give extraordinary game ideas the team they deserve.

Every project starts with a bold question: "What would make a player feel something they've never felt before?" That question drives every decision we make, from pixel to launch.

// History

Our Journey
From Zero to Legend

2018

The Studio is Founded

PixelForge begins with 6 people, a shared Figma board, and one shared obsession: making games that matter.

2019

First Title Ships — Dragon Byte

Our debut mobile title Dragon Byte reaches 500K downloads in its first month, earning us our first BAFTA nomination.

2021

Console Porting Division Launches

We open our Tokyo office and begin offering expert console porting services. First client: a mid-sized EU indie studio porting to Switch.

2023

Pixel Knights & Cyber Nexus Launch

Back-to-back critically acclaimed releases. Pixel Knights wins Game of the Year (Mobile) at the Gamescom Awards. Team grows to 85.

2025

Neon Drifter X — Our Masterpiece

Our most ambitious project to date ships to a 9.4 Metacritic score and 1.2M concurrent players at launch. Team now 120+.

// MILESTONE

18M+ Players Worldwide

Across all platforms and titles, our games have been played by over 18 million players in 120+ countries.

// What We Stand For

Our Values

These aren't taglines. They're the design principles we apply to every project, every meeting, every pixel.

Intentionality Over Output

We'd rather ship one great game than three average ones. Every feature, system, and line of code exists for a deliberate reason.

Radical Transparency

With clients and with each other. No surprises, no politics. We communicate problems early and solutions immediately.

Craft as Obsession

We are never satisfied with "good enough." The gap between good and legendary is where we live and where our best work happens.

Diverse Perspectives Win

The best games speak to everyone. Our team spans 22 nationalities because diverse perspectives produce richer, deeper experiences.

Bias Toward Shipping

Iteration beats perfection. We ship, we learn, we improve. Velocity with vision — not speed without direction.

Player-First Thinking

Every design decision starts with one question: "Does this make the player's experience better?" If not, it doesn't ship.
