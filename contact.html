// FAQs

Common Questions

How long does a typical project take?
Timelines vary significantly by scope. A mobile indie title typically takes 8–14 months. A PC/console AA project runs 18–36 months. Console porting for a single platform typically takes 4–8 months depending on the game's complexity and certification requirements. We'll give you a realistic estimate during the discovery phase.
Do you sign NDAs before discussing a project?
Absolutely. We sign a mutual NDA before any detailed project discussion begins. Your intellectual property is sacred. We take confidentiality seriously and our NDA process is fast — usually signed the same day.
What is the minimum project size you accept?
Our minimum engagement size is $50,000 for full development projects and $15,000 for porting work. For art-only or audio-only engagements, we scope on a case-by-case basis. We're happy to work with indie studios — we were one ourselves.
Do you retain any IP rights on games you develop?
No. All IP created during a client engagement belongs entirely to you. We work on a work-for-hire basis. You own 100% of the game, code, assets, and everything we produce together. This is non-negotiable for us.
Can I see examples of your past work?
Yes. Our public portfolio includes all published titles on the Games page. For client work under NDA, we can share case studies privately after signing an NDA with you. Just mention this in your enquiry and we'll arrange it during our first call.
Do you offer post-launch support and live ops?
Yes — it's one of our core service offerings. We can provide dedicated post-launch teams for bug fixes, patch deployment, DLC production, seasonal content, live events, and ongoing performance optimisation. We'll scope this as part of your initial agreement.
